Packaged paper data
==================

Each dataset has separate 100000.parquet, 200000.parquet, 400000.parquet and
800000.parquet files. Each file contains exactly the ordered series specified
by that scale's manifest in configs/cohorts. Shared series are intentionally
duplicated across scales. Full train, validation and test ranges are retained.
These are forecasting subsets, not the full populations needed to recompute
population-level TSF proportions.
